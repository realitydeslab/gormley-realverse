# Notes: Antony Gormley — INEXTRICABLE (Seoul, 2 Sep – 8 Nov 2025; White Cube + Thaddaeus Ropac)

Theme: "the relationship between humanity and the city has become inextricable ... this world now builds us."

## Series in the show -> digital translation
| Series | Works | Physical | Digital rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beamer | BIG SLEW, BIG FORM III, PLUCK 2 | mild steel bar, beams on 3 Cartesian axes | lattice lines along x/y/z clipped to body interior |
| Blockwork | SWERVE IV, COTCH XIII, STAY I-III | cast iron, stacked orthogonal blocks | voxel blocks inside body, sizes vary with depth |
| Open Blockwork | OPEN DAZE, HOME, EARTH | 8mm Corten plate, open cellular frame | cell edges on the body surface -> thin plates |
| Knotwork / Strapwork | Knotworks, DWELL, NOW, HERE | Corten ribbons, looping, plumbing/circuitry | axis-aligned random walks confined to body |
| Bunker | RETREAT: SLUMP | hollow block with square opening | (not in v1) |

Postures: standing (SWERVE), seated/perched (COTCH), crouching pressed to surfaces (STAY), lying.
