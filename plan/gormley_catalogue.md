# Antony Gormley — visual catalogue (from antonygormley.com/works/sculpture/series, viewed 2026-09-27)

72 series listed. Grouped here by *generative rule* (how the body is translated into material),
because that is what a digital system can reproduce.

## A. Orthogonal mass (room/Cartesian grid, solid)
| Series | Visual rule seen in photos | Element scale vs body |
|---|---|---|
| Blockworks 2003–07, Early Blockworks | dense aggregate of many small cast-iron blocks, 3 axes, protruding | 1–6 cm, hundreds–thousands |
| Cast Blockworks 2005–26, Aggregate Blockworks 2018–19 | medium blocks, loose, gaps, pieces jut out | 3–15 cm |
| Big Blockworks 2013–25, Raised, Double, Massive, Big Double, Big Stone | **10–25 large blocks stacked in courses**; figure reduced to a column | course height 8–25 cm |
| Plaster Works, Cube Works, Lead Blockworks | same as Big Blockworks, other materials / tumbled cubes | large |
| Bunkers 2022–26 | pale concrete big blocks, **one small square dark aperture** at the head | very large |
| Constructed/Cast Slabworks 2015–21, Extrusions 2015 | few plates/slabs, elongated along one axis | large |
| Blockworks: Wrestling with Modernism | 5–10 blocks compressed into a cube | very large |

## Staging common to all photos
- White gallery walls, pale polished concrete floor, soft overhead daylight, very soft contact shadows.
- Camera at eye level to chest height, long lens (~50–85 mm), frontal or 3/4, figure 1/2–2/3 of frame height.
- Materials: graphite cast iron, rust Corten (orange with dark mottling), grey mild steel, pale concrete, stainless.
